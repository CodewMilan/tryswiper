# Content Quality

Swiper should optimize for useful, original content rather than blindly optimizing for engagement.

## Hook

A good first slide should create a clear reason to continue.

Avoid:
- fake statistics
- fake urgency
- empty superlatives
- rage bait
- generic AI phrases

## Narrative

Each slide should advance the idea.

The last slides can naturally introduce:
- the product
- proof
- use case
- CTA

The product should not feel artificially inserted into unrelated educational content.

## Factuality

Never invent:
- customer numbers
- revenue
- awards
- testimonials
- product capabilities
- research
- statistics

If a claim is missing, ask for it or phrase the slide without the claim.

The implemented checker uses heuristics, not fact verification. Percentages, quantified social proof, guarantees and revenue multipliers require an exact matching supplied proof clause; empty or unrelated proof cannot authorize them. Supplied proof still needs human verification. Local provider fallback uses brief-derived copy and visibly marked draft scaffolding, with unfinished-copy warnings.

Text-fit checks use the native export fonts and wrapping rules to flag clipping, including explicit line breaks and long words. Structural checks also reject unsupported visuals and unavailable assets. Export remains a user choice; Pro preparation requires errors to be resolved for the specific saved version.

## Originality

Reference formats can inspire structure, but generated posts should use original copy and assets.

## Human feel

Prefer:
- specific details
- concrete examples
- strong nouns and verbs
- varied sentence lengths
- product-specific observations

Avoid:
- “game-changing”
- “revolutionary”
- “unlock your potential”
- excessive em dashes
- repetitive three-part AI phrasing
